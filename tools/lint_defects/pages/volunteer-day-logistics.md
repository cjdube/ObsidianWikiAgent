# Volunteer Day Logistics

**Summary**: Sign-up sheets and minibus transport for the annual company volunteer day, recorded in the same source that covered prompt caching.
**Sources**: source.md
**Last updated**: 2026-06-16

---

## Sign-up

The sheet opens six weeks ahead and closes when the minibus is full. Late
sign-ups go on a reserve list (source: source.md).

## Transport

One minibus, leaving at 08:30 and returning around 16:00. Anyone driving
separately should say so on the sheet so the seat is not held.

## What to bring

Sturdy shoes, a packed lunch, and a waterproof. Tools are supplied on site.
