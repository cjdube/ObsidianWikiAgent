# Dental Plan Enrollment

**Summary**: Deadlines and form links for the annual dental benefits enrollment window, filed from a source that also covered context window limits.
**Sources**: source.md
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

---

## Window

Enrollment opens on the first working day of the month and closes twenty-one
days later. Nothing can be changed outside that window except after a
qualifying life event (source: source.md).

## Tiers

Three tiers: single, single plus one, and family. The middle tier is the one
most people pick and the one most often filled in wrong.

## Forms

The election form and the dependant form are separate. Submitting only the
first is the single most common reason an enrollment is rejected.
