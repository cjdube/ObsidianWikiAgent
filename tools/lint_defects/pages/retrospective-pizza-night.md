# Retrospective Pizza Night

**Summary**: Catering and room booking for the team's monthly retrospective social, captured from the same notes as the agent evaluation write-up.
**Sources**: source.md
**Last updated**: 2026-07-28

---

## Booking

The upstairs room is held on the last Thursday of the month. Book it two weeks
ahead; it is the only room with enough seating (source: source.md).

## Food

Two large vegetarian, two large meat, one gluten-free. Orders go in by 15:00 so
delivery lands as the session ends.

## Who organises it

The rota rotates monthly. Whoever ran the previous month hands over the room
booking reference and the order history.
